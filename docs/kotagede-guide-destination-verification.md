# Kotagede Guide Destination Verification

Last checked: 2026-10-24

This record defines the evidence and visitor-access assumptions used by the
Stage 1 walking routes. Coordinates are stored as longitude, latitude. A place
being listed here does not imply unrestricted entry: homes, places of worship,
cemeteries, and residential lanes remain subject to signs, ceremonies, and
instructions from residents or attendants.

The three published walks begin at Salim Silver and finish at their final
destination: Royal Mataram Walk at Between Two Gates, Kotagede Heritage Walk
at Museum Kotagede, and Kotagede Food Walk at Kipo Bu Djito. Their displayed
distance and walking time cover the outward route only. The Royal Mataram
complex keeps Masjid Gedhe, Makam Raja, and Sendang Seliran together before
Between Two Gates; this is the one exception to ascending Salim-origin
distance order. The Heritage route then continues through Omah UGM and Gang
Soka to the museum. Its mapped public-street approach briefly retraces the
Masjid Besar corridor after Between Two Gates before heading west.

The paths reuse pedestrian geometry previously routed against
OpenStreetMap/Valhalla. The automated data test requires every stop to be
within 30 metres of a route vertex and each path to end beside its declared
finish, rather than at Salim Silver.

## Stage 2 admission gate

Candidates remain out of the published routes until all three checks pass:
exact location, current visitor access, and a safe pedestrian route that fits
the route's purpose. Verified royal archaeology stops would extend Kotagede
Heritage Walk, keeping the guide at three route groups.
